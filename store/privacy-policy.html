Privacy Policy

Italian Words: Learn & Play — last updated 20 October 2026

Short version

The app works offline and has no account. Your progress, saved words and settings stay on your phone only. We do not collect your name, e-mail, phone number or location.

What stays on your device

  • Your chosen interface language, dark mode and audio speed.
  • Your daily streak, quiz scores and “my words” list.
  • A random number created at first launch, used only to make your ten words of the day different from other users'. It is not linked to you and never leaves the phone.
  • Whether you bought “remove ads”.

All of this is deleted when you uninstall the app, or when you tap Reset progress in Settings.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Before any ad is requested the app shows Google's consent form (and, on iPhone, Apple's tracking prompt). Ads are only personalised if you agree. You can change your choice at any time with Change ads consent in Settings, or remove ads entirely with the one-time purchase. Google's own privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

The single purchase (“remove ads”) is handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details. The app only stores a yes/no flag on your device.

Optional online search

If the offline dictionary has no match, you may tap Search online. Only the word you typed is sent to the public Italian Wiktionary API. Nothing else is sent, and the feature is never used unless you tap the button.

Notifications

The daily reminder is off by default. If you turn it on, the notification is scheduled by your phone; no server is involved.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer address shown on the app's store page.
